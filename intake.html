Standard Drop-Off Policy
For drop-offs of 25 items or less

  • Dandelion Kids will decide what to buy and set the price.
  • You will not receive an itemized list.
  • You can pick up leftover clothing within 7 days, or opt to donate to our local partners.
Bulk Drop-Off Policy
For drop-offs of 26 items or more

I am agreeing to sell my clothing to Dandelion Kids for $0.50 per item of clothing, and $0.25 per onesie or baby pants.

  • Dandelion Kids will decide what to buy.
  • You will not receive an itemized list.
  • All remaining items will be donated — no pickup option for bulk drop-offs.

★ Store credit (if you choose it) will expire after ONE YEAR for either drop-off type.
