Module 1 · The Big Picture

What this app does

It tracks bags of kids' clothing through every step — from drop-off, to pricing, to paying the customer.

Why we use it
Before, we used a Google Form + spreadsheet. Now it's all in one place: who dropped off, what they brought, what we offered, and whether they got paid.
Module 1 · The Big Picture

The 7 stages

Every drop-off moves through these in order. Your job is to push them along.

1. NewA bag was dropped off
2. TaggingWe sort & label items
3. PricingWe set prices
4. Awaiting ReplyOffer email sent
5. Ready to PayCustomer responded
6. Awaiting PickupIf they want leftovers
7. ResolvedDone!

Step 6 of 7 · Pay

Store credit?

No Venmo needed. Record it in the credit log (Colleen will show you), then tap Mark Paid.

Why credit pays more
Store credit comes with a +60% bonus because it brings them back. Cash is the smaller amount.
Step 7 of 7 · Wrap up

Pickup or done?

After Mark Paid, the card goes one of two places.

Awaiting Pickup
If they're coming back for leftovers. Tap Customer Picked Up ✓ when they show up. After 7 days no-show: Donate Leftovers.
Resolved
If donating leftovers (or bulk drop), goes straight to Resolved. Done.

Module 8 · Special Cases

Don't do these

Things that go wrong if you're not careful.

Don't Mark Paid before paying
Always Venmo first.
Don't skip the paste step
Without Ctrl+V, customer gets a blank email.
Don't delete to "start over"
Use Manual Resolution or Back to Edit instead.
Don't ignore red cards
Red & pulsing = overdue. Deal with those first.
