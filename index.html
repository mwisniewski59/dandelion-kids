How it works
01

They drop a bag

Your own drop-off link — on a QR code by the counter or on your website. The customer agrees to your policy and fills in their details in about 90 seconds.

02

You price it

The bag appears on your board. Tag it, price it with your own rules, and the cash and store-credit amounts are worked out for you.

03

They choose

The customer gets a link with two numbers: cash, or a larger store credit. They pick, and say what to do with anything you didn't buy.

04

You pay out

The bag moves to Ready to Pay with the exact amount and method. Mark it paid and it's closed, with a record you can search later.

Why shops use it

Nothing ages out of sight

Every bag carries its age on the board — green today, red when it's been sitting too long. You can see the backlog from across the room.

Your prices, your rules

Set your own tiers, item types, and cash/credit split once. Everyone on the counter then prices the same way, whether it's their first week or their fifth year.

Your shop is your own

Each shop's data is walled off in the database itself, not just hidden in the app. Staff, managers, and owners each see only what they should.
